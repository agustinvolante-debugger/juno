Training — your split is fine, here's the load

You don't need a new routine. Legs+abs / chest-shoulders-triceps / back-biceps across five days works. What matters in a deficit is keeping the weight on the bar — that's the signal that tells your body to burn fat instead of muscle.

How to pick the weight — the only method you need

Leave 1–2 reps in the tank on every working set. If you're doing 8 and you could have done 10–11 with good form, the weight is right. If you could have done 14, it's too light. If you fail at 6, too heavy.

That's it. It self-corrects as you get stronger and it works on every exercise without you tracking percentages.

Three mistakes that cost you muscle while cutting
  • Dropping the weight and raising the reps to "tone." There is no toning. Light weight in a deficit tells your body it doesn't need the muscle.
  • Adding endless cardio. It eats recovery and appetite control. Walking is the exception — see below.
  • Doing abs to burn belly fat. Abs build the muscle under the fat. Train them for the look later; they won't reveal themselves.

Progression

When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets with 1–2 in reserve, add weight next session (2.5–5 lb upper body, 5–10 lb lower) and drop back to the bottom of the range. In a deficit, expect this to slow down by month three. Maintaining your lifts at 186 lb is a win — it means everything you lost was fat.

Walking — the free lever

8,000–10,000 steps a day. This burns more over a week than your gym sessions do and costs nothing in recovery. It's the single most underrated part of this plan.

Chile is one of the world's biggest mayo consumers per head — and it's the most calorie-dense thing in a normal kitchen. Two tablespoons on a sandwich is 190 calories you didn't taste as calories.

Three swaps that change nothing about how food tastes
  • Mayo → mustard, or Greek yogurt + lemon + garlic. On a completo or churrasco, mustard and pebre instead of mayo saves ~200 cal.
  • Creamy dressing → balsamic + a measured teaspoon of olive oil. Saves 100+.
  • Sour cream → 0% Greek yogurt. Identical texture, adds protein instead of fat.

The candy problem — budget it, don't ban it

Cold turkey fails at week three. Give yourself 150–200 calories a day of whatever you want and it fits inside the 2,450 with no damage.

  • Don't keep it in the house. This is 80% of the fix. You'll eat what is within reach at 11pm and nothing else.
  • Buy the single, not the bag. The bag always gets finished.
  • Good swaps: sugar-free jelly, frozen berries, a square of dark chocolate, diet soda, protein ice cream.
  • Eat protein at breakfast. Sugar cravings drop hard when the first meal is 40g+ of protein instead of carbs.

The one night out

400ml of spirits = 9 standard drinks ≈ 900 calories, and with sugary mixers plus late-night food a night runs to ~2,200. That's 10 lb of your 19 lb goal, from one night a week.

The calories aren't even the worst part: fat burning stops for 12–24 hours, deep sleep is wrecked for two nights, and cortisol from bad sleep parks fat on the belly specifically. Then you lose Sunday's training and your step count.

The budget version — you keep the night:
  • Cap at 150ml (~3.5 drinks). ~330 cal instead of 900.
  • Zero-calorie mixers only — soda water, diet coke, lime. Saves 600–800 on its own.
  • Eat 40g of protein before you go out. You drink less and you skip the 2am food.
  • Bank it: eat ~200 fewer calories on Thursday and Friday and the night costs you nothing.

Doing just this is worth ~8.5 lb by December — and you keep Sunday.

Tracking — so you know by week 3, not week 12

  • Weigh daily, same time, after the bathroom, before eating. Take the weekly average — daily numbers swing 3–4 lb on water and mean nothing.
  • The rule: if the weekly average hasn't moved in two consecutive weeks, cut 150 calories/day. Don't touch it before then.
  • Waist at the navel, once a week. This moves when the scale stalls, and it's the number you actually care about.
  • Photos, same light, every 4 weeks. You won't see the change in the mirror; you'll see it in August vs October.
  • Log the lifts. If weights hold while bodyweight falls, you're losing fat and keeping muscle. That's the whole game.
