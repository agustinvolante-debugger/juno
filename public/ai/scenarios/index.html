scenarios

Ways this breaks

Not predictions. Each one names the trigger that would start it, the observable that would tell you it had, and the path the shock takes through the stack — so you can check it yourself rather than take the argument on faith.

  • already happening

    The depreciation reset

    What happens to hyperscaler earnings if the assumed useful life of an AI server compresses back toward four years?

    tripwire · A useful-life change disclosed in the accounting-policy note of a Microsoft, Alphabet or Meta 10-K or 10-Q, with the dollar effect on depreciation quantified in the same note.

  • material

    Power arrives late

    What if the machines are bought, the contracts are signed, and the electricity to run them is three years behind schedule?

    tripwire · A hyperscaler or neocloud attributing a capacity or revenue shortfall to energisation delay on an earnings call or in a filing — or ERCOT's Batch Zero transmission plan, due fall 2027, pushing its cohort past 2030.

  • contested

    A compute buyer cannot pay

    What if one of the small number of counterparties underwriting the buildout cannot fund its commitments, and the sellers have to reprice a backlog built on one name?

    tripwire · A contract modification, deferral or impairment disclosed against a named large customer by Oracle, Microsoft, CoreWeave or Nebius — or a fall in Oracle's remaining performance obligations not explained by delivery.

  • low

    Taiwan interdiction

    What breaks, in what order, if the island holding most of the world's advanced-process capacity stops shipping — and what can this corpus honestly say about the cost?

    tripwire · TSMC moving a Taiwan shipment interruption from a risk factor to a current-period disclosure in a 20-F or 6-K. Absent that, the only published proxy is Taiwan's advanced-capacity share failing to fall on its projected 2027 path.
