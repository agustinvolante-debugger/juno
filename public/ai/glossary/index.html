24 terms, defined once

Defined as data rather than prose, so a term cannot be explained two ways in two places and drift. Several entries exist because the distinction they draw is one this site had to correct in its own work.

Capital expenditurecapex
Cash spent on long-lived physical assets — land, buildings, servers, transformers — rather than on running the business day to day.
why it matters hereThe whole AI buildout is a capex story. It hits cash immediately but earnings only gradually, through depreciation, which is why the gap between the two is the argument.
08 Cloud & Neoclouds16 Capital & Policy
Committed spend agreementMACC · committed spend · cloud commitment
A customer's contractual promise to spend a minimum amount with a cloud provider over several years.
why it matters hereThe real distribution moat. An eligible marketplace purchase can draw down 100% of the commitment, which moves procurement far more than a low marketplace fee does.
14 Distribution & GTM
Consignment
An arrangement where the customer owns the components and the manufacturer is paid only to assemble them.
why it matters hereIt never enters the assembler's revenue or costs, so identical work reports a higher margin on a smaller base. Nobody discloses the mix, which contaminates every margin comparison in the systems layer.
07 Systems & Integration
CoWoSchip-on-wafer-on-substrate · advanced packaging
TSMC's advanced packaging process, which bonds logic dies and memory stacks onto a single substrate.
why it matters hereThe chokepoint moved here from wafer fabrication. TSMC's own CEO says packaging capacity is limiting his customers' growth.
04 Fab & Tooling
Debt-service coverage ratioDSCR
Cash available to pay debt, divided by the debt payments due. A covenant sets the minimum.
why it matters hereCoreWeave's 1.15x covenant binds on cash flow, not asset value — so it is the first thing a demand pause would touch.
16 Capital & Policy
Depreciation
Spreading the cost of an asset across the years it is expected to be useful, instead of expensing it all at once.
why it matters hereThe single most consequential estimate in the stack. Assume a GPU lasts six years instead of five and billions of dollars of operating income appear, without anything physical changing.
08 Cloud & Neoclouds16 Capital & Policy
Extreme ultraviolet lithographyEUV · Low-NA EUV · High-NA EUV
The only production technique for printing the smallest chip features, sold by exactly one company.
why it matters hereASML ships on the order of 65 Low-NA systems a year. That integer is the physical ceiling on how fast leading-edge capacity can grow.
04 Fab & Tooling
Gross margin vs operating margin
Gross margin is revenue less the direct cost of delivery. Operating margin subtracts everything else it takes to run the company.
why it matters hereComparing one to the other flatters whoever gets the gross figure. This site cut a headline for doing exactly that.
06 Memory & Interconnect05 Compute Silicon
High-bandwidth memoryHBM
DRAM stacked vertically and wired directly to a processor, trading cost for enormous memory bandwidth.
why it matters hereThe scarce input inside the scarce input. It is why a 'commodity' memory maker ran a 72% operating margin.
06 Memory & Interconnect
Interconnection queue
The waiting list to connect a new generator or a large load to the electricity grid.
why it matters hereThe bottleneck inverted: the queue that matters is now for large loads, not new generation. ERCOT's is about 5.1 times its all-time peak demand.
02 Grid & Interconnect
Intersegment revenue
Revenue one division of a company books from another division.
why it matters hereAbout 95% of Intel Foundry's revenue is Intel selling to Intel — the number that decides whether it is a merchant foundry yet.
04 Fab & Tooling
Model Context ProtocolMCP
An open protocol for connecting models to external tools and data, donated to the Linux Foundation in December 2025.
why it matters hereThe clearest test of absorption: the protocol's own steward published research showing code execution collapsing its tool-calling overhead by 98.7%.
12 Middleware & Agent Infra
Nameplate capacity
The maximum rated output of a plant or project, as opposed to what it actually delivers or whether it gets built.
why it matters hereQueue totals are nameplate. Only a fraction of applicants ever sign an interconnection agreement — in one PJM cohort, 24%.
02 Grid & Interconnect01 Primary Energy
Neocloud
A cloud provider that rents GPUs and does essentially nothing else — CoreWeave, Nebius, Crusoe, Lambda.
why it matters hereThe hyperscaler business model without the other revenue to absorb a bad year, which makes it the layer's price-discovery mechanism.
08 Cloud & Neoclouds
Power purchase agreementPPA
A long-term contract to buy electricity at an agreed price, usually from a specific plant.
why it matters hereHow hyperscalers lock in power. Watch for 'up to' — a headline megawatt figure is often a ceiling on a development framework, not contracted supply.
01 Primary Energy
Remaining performance obligationRPO · backlog
Revenue a company has contracted for but not yet delivered or recognised.
why it matters hereThe best available evidence that AI demand is real rather than hoped for — but it measures obligations, not cash, and says nothing about cancellation terms.
08 Cloud & Neoclouds14 Distribution & GTM
Run rateARR · annualised revenue
A recent short period of revenue multiplied out to a year, as if it continued unchanged.
why it matters hereNot the same thing as annual revenue, and the difference is where most AI-revenue confusion lives. A company can have a $47bn run rate and a much smaller recognised year.
10 Foundation Models13 Applications
Slot reservation agreementSRA
A non-binding hold on a future manufacturing slot, reported separately from firm orders and from backlog.
why it matters hereMixing reservations into orders is how a 3.7x book-to-ship ratio gets reported as 7x.
01 Primary Energy
Source tiersT1 · T2 · T3 · tier 1
This site's grading of evidence: T1 is filings, regulators and primary company documents; T2 is quality press and research shops; T3 is named-expert transcripts.
why it matters here53% of the corpus carries at least one T1 source. Where a claim rests on something weaker, the page says so rather than hiding it.
Special purpose vehicleSPV · bankruptcy-remote subsidiary
A separate legal entity created to hold specific assets and debts, ring-fenced from its parent.
why it matters hereHow tens of billions of datacenter capex stays off the parent's balance sheet. Meta discloses $45.95bn of maximum exposure to loss against $1.83bn of carried equity in one such venture.

Steelman
The strongest version of the argument against your own position, stated by someone who holds it.
why it matters hereEvery verdict on this site is followed immediately by one, attributed. A verdict without a named counterargument is an opinion wearing evidence.
Tokenstoken · per-token pricing · MTok
The unit models read and write in — roughly a word fragment. Inference is sold per million of them.
why it matters hereThe stack's meter. Per-capability token prices fall roughly an order of magnitude a year while training costs rise, and that scissors defines the model layer.
11 Inference & Serving10 Foundation Models
Total processing performanceTPP
A compute metric US export rules use to decide whether a chip may be sold to a restricted destination.
why it matters hereThe January 2026 rule caps China exports at 50% of cumulative TPP shipped to US customers — a compute-weighted, lifetime measure, not a unit count.
16 Capital & Policy
Useful lifeserver life · asset life · depreciation schedule
How long a company assumes an asset will earn its keep — the number that sets the depreciation schedule.
why it matters hereIn January 2025 Amazon shortened server lives while Meta extended them. Same hardware, same quarter, opposite directions: the consensus broke in public.
