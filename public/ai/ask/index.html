how it works

  • Retrieval is lexical over an index built from the same commit as the content, so a page and its retrievable form cannot drift.
  • Below a stated relevance floor (BM25 evidence 8) no model is called at all — the refusal is decided before any answer exists to be tempted by.
  • Claims that failed this project's own attribution audit are quarantined: they are not on the site and not in the index, so they cannot be retrieved or cited.
  • It can still be wrong about what the corpus means. Follow the citation — that is what it is for. Full bibliography at /sources.
