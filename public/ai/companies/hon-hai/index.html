Hon Hai Precision (Foxconn)

The largest AI rack assembler on earth, and eight years of evidence that assembling racks confers no pricing power.

07 Systems & Integration

Hon Hai assembles more AI racks than anyone. It has not become more profitable per dollar in eight years — and the disclosure that would tell you whether that is even the right measurement does not exist.

1.Role in the stack

The largest single node in systems integration, and increasingly the seller of a whole rack rather than a box. Cloud and Networking Products were 48% of group revenue in the first quarter of 2026 [s], with AI servers above half of server revenue as a stated floor and no supporting table [s]. Spokesperson James Wu has claimed around 40% share of "the AI market" [o] — no market definition, no methodology, no corroboration. This page records it as his opinion.

Gross margin was 6.15% in 2025 against 6.27% in 2018, while revenue rose from NT$5.29tn to NT$8.10tn [s]. Revenue up by half; margin down twelve basis points. Operating margin did improve, 2.57% to 3.20% [s], and the first quarter of 2026 ran at 6.18% gross and 3.57% operating on NT$2.12tn of quarterly revenue [s]. The company also runs more than forty facilities across twelve US states [s] — its own claim, with no split of AI-rack output by geography.

One disclosure contaminates every margin comparison in this layer, including the three above, and it belongs here rather than in a footnote. Hon Hai's CFO said consignment is now the main transaction model in its ASIC-server projects [s]. Under consignment the customer owns the silicon, so the most expensive content in the rack never enters revenue or cost of sales — and identical physical work reports a higher gross margin on a much smaller base. No integrator discloses its buy-and-sell versus consignment mix. Every ratio on this page is measured against a variable nobody outside these companies can size.

3.The bull case

It belongs to rotating CEO Michael Chiang, and it is the strongest version available: pure assembly is a pass-through, so the escape route is owning the differentiated content. Hon Hai is expanding into liquid cooling, mechanical components, high-speed transmission, power modules and system validation to raise its self-manufacturing ratio [o]. The operating-margin improvement is what that looks like early [s], and the proof the destination exists sits in the same supply chain: Asia Vital Components took operating margin from 2.47% to 19.73% making cold plates [s], Delta from 7.66% to 15.13% in power [s].

forecast · Hon Hai, Michael Chiang, May 20262026 AI rack shipments guided to more than double, with the condition stated explicitly and doing real work — only if supply-chain material availability can meet demand [f]. Capex was around NT$174bn in 2025, up about 27%, guided to more than 30% growth in 2026 [s].

4.The bear case

Eight years of flat gross margin on 53% more revenue is the cleanest evidence anywhere that scale confers no pricing power in rack assembly [s]. Chiang describes many of these businesses as buy-and-sell models in which product prices are adjusted according to costs [o] — which protects absolute profit against input inflation and means the layer cannot absorb a cost shock, only transmit it into the buyer's capital budget one layer up.

The read-across from peers is worse than Hon Hai's own flat line. Quanta's AI margin expansion is a completed round trip: 4.78% in 2019, a 7.85% peak in 2024, 4.78% again in the first quarter of 2026 [s]. Wistron's revenue grew 108% in 2025 while gross margin fell from 8.01% to 6.13% [s]. And in-sourcing is a strategy, not a mechanism: Inventec rode the same wave and moved operating margin from 1.48% to 1.84% across seven years [s].

◆What to watch

Falsifiable indicators — each one resolves on a disclosure, not on an opinion.

  1. 01Whether consolidated gross margin ever leaves the 6.1-6.3% band in the next TWSE quarterly filing. Q1 2026 printed 6.18%; eight consecutive years have stayed inside forty basis points of that, so a break in either direction is new information.
  2. 02Whether Hon Hai discloses its buy-and-sell versus consignment mix. A single percentage would revalue every margin comparison in this layer, including the ones on this page — right now the CFO has told us the denominator is moving without telling us how far.
  3. 03Whether the self-manufacturing push shows up in operating margin while gross margin stays flat. That is the specific signature of in-sourcing components rather than repricing assembly, and it is the only way to tell Chiang's strategy from operating leverage.
  4. 04Whether 2026 AI rack shipments actually more than double. The guidance carries an explicit condition on supply-chain material availability, and the monthly TWSE revenue filings resolve it before the annual report does.
  5. 05Whether Cloud and Networking crossing half of group revenue coincides with any margin movement at all. It was 48% in Q1 2026; if the mix keeps climbing and the ratio does not, the pass-through thesis is confirmed by the company's own reporting.
