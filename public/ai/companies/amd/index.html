AMD

The credible second merchant supplier — which in October 2025 paid its largest prospective customer in equity to become one.

05 Compute Silicon

The most important document AMD filed in this cycle is not an earnings release. It is an 8-K describing a warrant that pays a customer to buy chips.

1.Role in the stack

AMD is the second merchant accelerator supplier: EPYC CPUs and Instinct GPUs, a part with a price list, sold to anyone. That position is genuinely valuable — a rationed market needs a second source — and it is priced like a second source, which is the whole tension on this page.

Q1 2026 revenue was $10.253bn [s], with the data center segment at $5.775bn, up 57% [s], at a 55% non-GAAP gross margin — 53% GAAP [s]. Q2 2026 was guided to approximately $11.2bn plus or minus $300m [f].

Two comparisons place it. AMD's entire data center segment [s] is smaller than NVIDIA's networking line alone, which was $14.8bn in the quarter ended April 2026 [s], and is under a tenth of NVIDIA's $75.2bn of quarterly data center revenue [s].

3.The bull case

Demand is contracted, at scale, by the buyer everyone wants. OpenAI agreed in October 2025 to deploy 6 GW of AMD GPUs over five years [s], with the first gigawatt of MI450 targeted for the second half of 2026 and a concurrent binding commitment to purchase it. A 57% data center growth rate [s] is what the pre-MI450 base looks like; the deal is not in the numbers yet.

AMD is also differentiating by system rather than by die. It announced a disaggregated inference solution with Cerebras in July 2026 claiming up to 5x higher tokens per second per watt [e] — a vendor claim on a vendor configuration, logged as one, and available through Cerebras Cloud rather than AMD's own channel.

4.The bear case

Read the price AMD paid. It issued OpenAI a warrant on 5 October 2025 for up to 160 million shares at $0.01 each, vesting in tranches tied to Instinct deployments and to share-price targets escalating to $600 for the final tranche [s]. Full vesting is contingent on the whole 6 GW [s]. The structure is a supplier issuing equity to secure demand — the mirror image of NVIDIA investing in customers, and arguably the weaker side of it, because NVIDIA is buying upside in the buyer while AMD is handing upside to the buyer.

The economics behind that decision are the second half of the bear case. A 55% non-GAAP gross margin [s] against NVIDIA's 74.9% GAAP [s] is roughly twenty points of structural difference that has not narrowed in a market where advanced packaging is sold out and lead times run 52-78 weeks [e]. If a second source cannot raise price when the first source cannot ship, the constraint on AMD is not capacity. It is software and system-level position — which is why the flagship differentiation claim of 2026 arrives through a partner's cloud [e] rather than through AMD's own rack.

◆What to watch

Falsifiable indicators — each one resolves on a disclosure, not on an opinion.

  1. 01Whether the first warrant tranche vests. It is contingent on the initial 1 GW of MI450 being deployed, targeted for the second half of 2026, and vesting shows up in AMD's share count and 8-K filings rather than in a press release.
  2. 02Whether non-GAAP gross margin moves off roughly 55%. A twenty-point structural gap to NVIDIA has not narrowed through the strongest demand environment either company will see; margin, not revenue, is the variable that would show AMD has priced up rather than in.
  3. 03Whether AMD's data center growth rate exceeds NVIDIA's in any single quarter. Both grew fast in Q1 2026 — 57% against 92% — so relative growth, not absolute revenue, is the share-shift test.
  4. 04Whether OpenAI is disclosed as a greater-than-10% customer in a subsequent AMD annual report. The warrant makes the customer a shareholder; the concentration disclosure is where that stops being a narrative and becomes an accounting fact.
  5. 05Whether any share-price tranche of the warrant is disclosed as vested. The final tranche is tied to $600 per share, so the schedule embeds AMD's own equity as the currency for the deal.
